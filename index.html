Opening Statement

The single agency doing more to keep San Franciscans safe — and at a fraction of the price — is the one the city is about to dissolve.

"[SF Pretrial is] the greatest thing no one's ever really heard of." — Toney Chaplin, Former Assistant Chief, SFPD (30 years)

Exhibit D · A Model for the Country

Other jurisdictions are flying in to study this program. San Francisco is killing it.

SF Pretrial isn't just exceptional in San Francisco — it's one of the most-studied community-based pretrial programs in the country. Counties from elsewhere are reaching out to adopt the model. The city that built it is about to dismantle it.

1 of 2

Community-based pretrial programs at this scale in the U.S.

SF Pretrial and NYC's Criminal Justice Agency. That's the list. Every other jurisdiction runs either a government-administered program or a far smaller operation.

OpenJustice

Software other counties are asking to adopt

SF Pretrial built the case-management platform the field didn't have — outcomes-first, wraparound-integrated. Counties across California and other states are already reaching out.

The trend line

The country is moving toward this model

Nationally, pretrial services are shifting toward community-based, independent supervision. SF Pretrial has been operating it for 50 years — long enough to have answered questions the field is only beginning to ask.

Exhibit F · The Law They're Citing Doesn't Apply

SB 129 explicitly exempts San Francisco.

The law being used to justify moving pretrial services into probation departments across California carved San Francisco out by name — because the program already exceeded the standard.

Footnote 6 · Year 3 Report to the Legislature

"San Francisco and Santa Clara counties are excluded from this program because their pretrial programs were established prior to, and operate at a level exceeding, the requirements of SB 129."

— Judicial Council of California, Pretrial Pilot Program Report

The Superior Court is invoking a law that, by its own administrative body's reading, doesn't apply here.
