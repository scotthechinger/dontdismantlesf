About these numbers. Counts are click counts from the live site at dontdismantlesf.org, not server-confirmed sends. The funnel is: a user opens the modal (Modal opened), then clicks one of the three send paths (Gmail, Mailto, Copy). Click-through-to-send is high once someone opens, so this is a strong proxy for emails sent.

Constant Contact signups are tracked separately in your Constant Contact dashboard — the "Updates card click" number is just the click on that card, not the actual signup.
